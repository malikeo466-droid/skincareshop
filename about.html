ABOUT SKIN1004

Our Story

Inspired by the pure nature of Madagascar, SKIN1004 creates gentle and effective skincare products made with Centella Asiatica. Our mission is to help people achieve healthy, balanced skin through clean ingredients and trusted formulas.

2012

The Beginning

SKIN1004 was founded with the vision of creating natural skincare products using high-quality Centella Asiatica from Madagascar.

2016

First Product

We launched our first Centella skincare collection, designed to soothe and protect sensitive skin.

2020

Global Expansion

Our products became available in many countries, earning the trust of customers around the world.

2026

Growing Together

We continue to develop innovative skincare solutions while maintaining our commitment to quality and sustainability.

Our Mission
